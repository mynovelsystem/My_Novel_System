Chapter 12: Pattern Recognition

Zorian woke up with a headache. The morning rays filtered through the window blinds.

He rubbed his temples, thinking about the repetitive pattern he had observed in the mana fluctuations around the academy.

Every morning was the same. The birds chirped at the exact same rhythm, and his sister was about to burst through the door in three, two, one...

"Zorian, wake up! Breakfast is ready!" she yelled, slamming the door open.

He sighed. The loop had indeed reset.
